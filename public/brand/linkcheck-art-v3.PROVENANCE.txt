Original App Composer cut-paper artwork — Link Check
Reviewed 17 October 2026.

Source: one generated raster, "tile-linkcheck-v3", produced through the fal.ai queue endpoint
openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image under MP's authorisation, request
01a147db-5c32-7a52-aa46-921868e021f2. The request itself, its prompt, the cost basis and the
review of every rejected image are recorded in the owning App Composer repository at
public/brand/apps/provenance.json and .artwork/tiles-direction-a.json. FAL_AI_API_KEY is read
from the environment by scripts/generate-app-art.mjs; it is never printed, never written to a
manifest, never committed and never shipped to the browser.

Art direction: App Composer cut-paper screenprint — warm ivory paper #f4f0e8, graphite ink
#252923, burnt orange #d95022, with teal #2f9fbd as this app's accent and signal orange #ff9b55
inside the lens. Flat shapes, ink-lined paper edges, coarse screenprint grain, deliberate
registration offset. No shading, perspective, gloss, gradients or glow. Subject: one teal paper
magnifier ring with a short straight handle lying over a pale ivory page card; inside the ring a
single burnt-orange paper rectangle shows one flat hill curve and one round sun, and two
graphite rules run across the card beneath it. The magnifier stands for inspection and is never
presented as a screenshot, a browser window or a platform result.

Files in this repository, all exported from the master at review time:
- linkcheck-art-v3-master.png — the reviewed master, square (1024px); byte-identical to the
  reviewed .artwork/masters/tile-linkcheck-v3.png recorded by App Composer.
- linkcheck-art-v3-480.webp and linkcheck-art-v3-960.webp — 480px and 960px page hero
  derivatives, one square tile with a reserved 1:1 layout box and a 1px hairline border.
- linkcheck-share-v3.jpg — 1200x630 JPEG social derivative. The same bytes are this app's card
  in the App Composer catalogue (public/brand/apps/linkcheck-share-v3.jpg there), so the product
  page and the catalogue describe the app with one card.
- linkcheck-share-v3.webp and linkcheck-share-v3-600.webp — 1200x630 and 600x315 WebP variants.
- apps/linkcheck-small-v3.webp — 96px navigation crop of the same square artwork, alongside the
  other four apps' crops for this app's own app switcher.

Dimensions are reserved in the markup. The illustration is explicitly separate from the
inspection: it is never presented as an inspected page, its preview or a platform acceptance
result.

mark.svg and icon.png are authored, not generated: hand-written SVG geometry of the page card,
lens, landscape panel and rules in the same paper grammar, plus a 512px flat ivory rendering of
that same mark for apple-touch-icon. No traced third-party mark, vendor icon or platform logo is
used.

Superseded in this release and removed from the repository: the 14 October 2026 glossy
linkcheck-art.jpg poster and its provenance note, which showed an orange glass lens over a
linked graphite object; the unreferenced composer-art.jpg and composer-mark.svg pair; and the
13 October standalone icon.svg, replaced by mark.svg and icon.png. Their bytes, prompts and the
original generated master path remain in this repository's git history; the reviewed App
Composer record keeps the comparison note.
